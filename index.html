Digital Mental Health Support Platform

A secure, confidential space for tertiary level students to complete mental health self-assessments, share recovery stories, and connect with licensed counselors through chat and video sessions.

Core Platform Features

Self-Assessments

Screen for anxiety, depression, bipolar disorder, PTSD, and stress with validated questionnaires.

Direct Chat

Message experienced counselors securely in real-time without page reloads.

Story Sharing

Share your own mental health recovery journey publicly or anonymously.

Video Consultations

Secure WebRTC video rooms prepped for online session consultations.

Benefits of Seeking Support

  • - Improved academic performance through better focus, concentration, and reduced stress levels.
  • - Stronger coping skills and emotional resilience to handle life's challenges confidently.
  • - Healthier relationships and improved communication with peers and family.
  • - Early detection and management of anxiety, depression, and other mental health conditions.
  • - Greater self-awareness, personal growth, and a more positive outlook on life.

Risks of Not Seeking Support

  • - Worsening symptoms of anxiety, depression, and burnout that become harder to treat over time.
  • - Declining academic performance, missed deadlines, and risk of academic suspension or dropout.
  • - Social isolation, strained relationships, and difficulty trusting others.
  • - Increased risk of substance abuse as an unhealthy coping mechanism.
  • - Long-term emotional damage and reduced quality of life that impacts career and future goals.
